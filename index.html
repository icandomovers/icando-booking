A smoother moving day

Four practical steps that help the crew start on time and protect the things you care about.

  1. 01Label the essentials

    Mark boxes you’ll want opened first at the new place.

  2. 02Set aside valuables

    Keep documents, medication, keys and small valuables with you.

  3. 03Clear the route

    Keep hallways, entrances and parking access open for the crew.

  4. 04Flag special items

    Let us know about fragile, oversized or unusually heavy pieces.
